# Community references

Text copies of three documents written by the Night of 100 Frights speedrunning community. They record what players observed while playing the retail builds. They are not reverse-engineered and are not target evidence: read [community references](../../../docs/community_references.md) before citing them. Sources and authorship are in [CREDITS](../../../CREDITS.md#community-references).

| File | Source | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| [`speedrunning-encyclopedia.md`](speedrunning-encyclopedia.md) | *Scooby-Doo! Night of 100 Frights Speedrunning Encyclopedia* (Google Doc, Markdown export) | Movement tech, skips and strategies per level, version differences, glitches, changelog |
| [`version-differences.csv`](version-differences.csv) | *Version Differences*, sheet "Differences" | Per-skip availability on PS2 1.0.1, PS2 Greatest Hits, Xbox, GameCube 1.0 and GameCube 1.1 |
| [`skips-glitch-dictionary.csv`](skips-glitch-dictionary.csv) | *Night of 100 Frights Skips and Glitch Dictionary*, sheet "Skips" | Skip name, discoverer, discovery date and video hosts |

Changes from the originals:

- The encyclopedia's embedded images (gameplay screenshots and box art) are removed and each is marked `*[Image omitted]*`. The three disc photos in "How to Tell if You Have GCN Version 0 or Version 1" are replaced with the ring codes they show. Video links are kept.
- The CSVs drop empty rows and trailing empty columns, and line breaks inside cells become spaces. The sheets' hyperlinks do not survive CSV export, so cells such as `Link`, `1,2` or `Imgur` are link labels; the URLs are in the original sheets.

The community's "GameCube Version 0" and "Version 1" (the sheet's "1.0" and "1.1") correspond to the disc ring codes `DOL-GIHE-0-00 USA` and `DOL-GIHE-0-01 USA`. This project's target is version 0 (`DOL-GIHE-USA`, `GIHE78` revision 0). Version 1 is reported to lock hanging objects such as chandeliers in place and to drop the Coast 3 "invisible fishnet"; all PAL GameCube copies are reported to be version 1.
