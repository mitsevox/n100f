# **Scooby-Doo\! Night of 100 Frights Speedrunning Encyclopedia**

This document includes much of the general speedrunning knowledge the community has developed for Night of 100 Frights over the years. It will be continually updated as we discover more about the game. However, not every detail about the game is included here, so if the information in this document doesn’t answer all your questions, please feel free as always to come and ask us in the Discord :) 

# **Speedrun Movement**

The fastest form of movement in Night of 100 Frights is Scooby’s Charge. You hold down the Run button while chaining together charges in order to prevent Scooby from skidding to a stop at the end of his charge.

However, Scooby reaches his top speed towards the end of his charges, so if you only have a short distance to travel, it is faster to just run than it is to start up a charge because that will actually slow Scooby down. It was calculated that after you start the charge, it takes 0.6 seconds for it to become faster than the normal run.

**Headbutt** 

When Scooby headbutts an enemy or object, he gets knocked back for a second and you can’t move until that animation is finished. However, there is a frame perfect trick that allows you to cancel the knock back animation by pressing the jump or charge button again at the exact moment you headbutt an enemy or object.

**Super Smash**

Scooby’s Super Smash ability moves him forward a small amount. You can use a Super Smash after a Double Jump to get the extra distance you need to make it across gaps that you would not normally be able to clear with a Double Jump alone.   
[https\://clips.twitch.tv/HeartlessHonestDragonflyMikeHogu](https://clips.twitch.tv/HeartlessHonestDragonflyMikeHogu)

**Long Jumps**

Another way to make it across gaps that you wouldn’t normally clear is by waiting to jump until after you begin falling off the edge of a platform. You can do this by walking, running, or charging off the edge if you have enough room to build up momentum. This can be used for all kinds of jumps, from ones as short as the [Chandelier Jump](#heading=h.7miu4vrxnsm) to ones as long as the [Warpless 600 Snack Gate Skip](#warpless-600-snack-gate-skip).  It is possible that charging before a Long Jump will let you travel a slightly farther distance.

**Types of Jumps**

Scooby has 3 different types of jumps:

- **A “Straight-Up” Jump** which is done by pressing the Jump button while standing still. The 2nd jump of the Double Jump always uses this type of jump.  
    
- **A “Curved” Jump** which is done by pressing the Jump button while moving but NOT holding down the Run button.  
    
- **A “Leap” Jump** which is done by pressing the Jump Button while moving and holding down the Run button.

Scooby’s hitbox changes slightly depending on the jump he does, so sometimes the type of jump you use can make a skip easier to pull off or an invisible ledge easier to hit. For example, [Barrel Jump](#barrel-jump) and [Shaggy Skip 2/Rubber Band Skip](#shaggy-skip-2-/-rubber-band-skip) are easier when you use a Curved Jump.

**D-Pad Strats**

When trying to clip through walls, it was discovered that using the D-pad to control Scooby instead of the Analog Stick puts him at the perfect angle needed to do the clip. This can be useful in multiple strats such as [SCS](#skull-cliff-skip-\(scs\)), [All Scares Void Warp](#all-scares-void-warp), and [Phase 1 Skip](#mastermind-phase-1-skip).

**Invincibility Frames**

After taking damage, Scooby will get a few seconds of invincibility. These Invincibility Frames can be used to get past obstacles that would otherwise hurt him. Most notably, it can be used to walk across water without dying in some [Coast Boats](#coast-boats) strats. Another thing to note is that Scooby’s Invincibility Frames last longer when he is hit by an obstacle like a falling rock or thorns than when he is hit by an enemy.

**Ice Physics and Conveyor Belt Momentum**

Throughout the game, there are a few levels with Icy or Slippery Floors that cause Scooby to slide around. There are also Conveyor Belts and Moving Platforms that can push Scooby around. Here is a basic explanation of these properties and how they change Scooby’s momentum and also how to deal with them. (Time Stamp 13:17 and 15:07) [https\://youtu.be/RU5SPPvjqxA?t=797](https://youtu.be/RU5SPPvjqxA?t=797)

**Water Properties**

Water hazards will act differently when it's your first time entering the level as opposed to after you die and respawn in the level. On your first time entering the level, it is possible for Scooby to land on a rock or boat while the water is rising and not die to the water. If you try to do this after you die and respawn, the water will kill you instead. This explains why you can stand on the boat in the [Unsinkable Sinking Boat](#the-unsinkable-sinking-boat) glitch after it has been submerged. This can also explain how we are able to Void Warp through water. 

[https\://www\.youtube.com/watch?v=shQO\_RvMJfY](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=shQO_RvMJfY)

So if you are trying to Void Warp through water, but have already died in a level, it may help to leave and re-enter so you can regain this property.

# **Pause Storage**

Pause Storage is a glitch that allows you to access a sort of debug menu. From this menu, you can warp from any location in the game to a Warp Gate that you have previously activated. This is achieved by unplugging the controller while doing specific menuing on the pause screen. Currently, this is only doable on Gamecube, but an alternate version has been found on PS2.

**Softlock Escape:** As long as you have picked up the Map from Holly (using the All Inventions cheat codes **will not** give you the map), you can use this glitch to get out of any softlock.

# **Fade Skip**

After you press the Enter prompt to enter a level in the game, the screen will fade to black before the load screen appears. A way has been found to skip this fadeout and go directly to the loading screen. If you unplug the controller and then quit the game right at the end of the fade, you can start a new game and the fade out will be skipped for the first level that you enter. This will work on any level you choose to enter, but for runs, it is most commonly used while entering the Playground. This skip will only work for 1 level load. If you want to do it again, you'll have to do the setup and quit the game again.

[https\://www\.youtube.com/watch?v=WzixitBExmc](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WzixitBExmc)

# **Console Versions**

Night of 100 Frights was released for Gamecube, Playstation 2, and Xbox.

- The Gamecube version can be played on a Gamecube or Wii console.  
    
- The Xbox version can only be played on an original Xbox. **It can NOT be played on an Xbox 360\.**  Playing on Xbox 360 causes the game to crash during the Mastermind fight therefore preventing you from finishing the run.  
    
- PAL versions only exist for the Gamecube and PlayStation 2\. The Xbox version was never released in Europe.

**Gamecube Version 0 VS Version 1**

**To our knowledge**, all Playstation 2 and Xbox games are the same. However, there are 2 versions of the Gamecube game that have major differences when it comes to speedrunning. **Version 0** has Free Chandeliers and the Invisible Fishnet whereas **Version 1** has Locked Chandeliers and no Invisible Fishnet. **All PAL Gamecube copies of the game are Version 1\.**

**Free Chandeliers VS Locked Chandeliers**

**Free Chandeliers** means that you can swing in a very large radius when hanging from a chandelier, life preserver, tire swing, ect. This allows us to do certain skips like [100% Greenhouse Early](#100%-greenhouse-early).

**Locked Chandeliers** means that any item you can hang from is basically locked into place and prevents you from swinging much at all. This makes it impossible to use the chandelier strat for 100% Greenhouse Early on this version. 

**Invisible Fishnet**

This is a glitch in Coast 3 that allows us to reach [Coast Boats](#coast-boats) early. The intended way to reach the boats is by Super Smashing a button which moves a fish net into place, allowing you to grab onto it and jump over to the boats. However, on certain versions of the game, the fish net is always there even if you haven’t pushed the button. It is invisible, but Scooby can still grab on to it. Using the Invisible Fishnet is the fastest way to do Coast Boats.

Because of these things, Version 0 is considered more optimal, but if you don’t have that version, there are alternative strats you can use that don’t lose much time. (These are listed in the “Skips and Strats'' section under their respective skips.)

# **Load Times**

Playstation 2 has the slowest load times of all the versions while Gamecube has the fastest. Technically Xbox has faster loading screens, but the fade out when you leave a level is slower than any other version, making the time you wait between levels on Xbox about equal to Gamecube. However, since Gamecube has the ability to do strats like the [0 Cycle Coast Boxes](#coast-1-key-boxes), Optimal [Coast Boats](#coast-boats), and [Plunger Skip](#plunger-skip), it is considered the most optimal version for speedrunning most categories of Night of 100 Frights.

The Playstation 2 version also needs to wait the longest before it can skip cutscenes whereas Xbox can skip cutscenes the fastest.

## **Hallway Snack Boxes** {#hallway-snack-boxes}

*[Image omitted]*

It is possible to reach the chandeliers in the hallway of Clamor 1 and collect the Snack Boxes before you get the Springs by using invisible walls. You can hit pieces of collision on the wall where the wooden trim meets the blue wall paper or on the frames of the paintings and then grab on to the chandeliers. These are another quick 5 or 10 snacks.

**Location:** Clamor in the Manor 1

## **Lighthouse Staircase Movement** {#lighthouse-staircase-movement}

*[Image omitted]*

In Lighthouse 1, you helmet a button at the bottom of the Lighthouse which raises the light containing the Super Smash up to the top. You need to follow it up by climbing the winding staircase. You want to time the cycle so that you don’t have to wait for the panel to open, but so that you also aren’t late to reach it. This is done by pushing the button (you can go for the knockback animation skip here), then collecting the Snack Boxes in the boxes, and then running up the staircase while Charging past every monster in the way. You can do this by Charging towards the railing as you go past them, but still angled enough to keep you moving forward.

There are 2 ways to do this. The safer cycle is to collect 2 or more Snack Boxes before you push the button. This gives you more time to reach the top, so if you accidentally bonk a monster on the way up, you will still make the cycle. The more risky cycle is to collect only 1 Snack Box before pushing the button. This makes the timing more tight, so if you bonk an enemy, you may not reach the top in time.

**Inventions Needed:** Springs, Helmet

## **Coffin Snacks Explained** {#coffin-snacks-explained}

*[Image omitted]*

There are 4 Coffins in Gloom and Doom 2 that open when you push their corresponding button. Each Coffin has 4 Snack Boxes inside them. This is how Coffin RNG works for when they spit out snacks:  
Snacks will only come out of one Coffin each time you enter the room. So let’s say there are snacks that come out of the 1st Coffin. No snacks will come out of the 2nd, 3rd, or 4th Coffins until you reenter the room. Also, each time you enter the room, there is always one Coffin that is guaranteed to have snacks. However, if that Coffin has already given you snacks, it will not give you any more, so neither snacks nor a Ghost will come out of it. So it is possible to open every Coffin in one visit and not get any snacks.   
When you need snacks from all 4 Coffins, you have a 100% chance of getting snacks from one of the Coffins. When you need 3 Coffins, you have a 75% chance of getting snacks because the game could choose to give you snacks from the Coffin that you already got snacks from. When you need 2 Coffins, you have a 50% chance and when there’s only 1 Coffin left, you have a 25% chance.

# **Void Warps**

Void Warping means that you fall out of bounds or fall past the Death Plane and into the void, so the game warps you back to whatever area is flagged as the entrance to that room. This is not always the door that you entered the room from. 

Most Void Warps involve you taking damage on a Flying Fish and then clipping beneath the water. We believe that it is possible to clip under the water because of something to do with the tide moving up and down, so it is like a moving Death Plane. It is also believed that the Death Plane opens up for a split second as a platform or Flying Fish goes under the water, so if you take damage as they go underwater, you could slip through the Death Plane and Void Warp.

## **Hedge 1 Gate VS Skull Cliff Skip Gate** {#hedge-1-gate-vs-skull-cliff-skip-gate}

The reason as to why Hedge Skip is so inconsistent is in the properties of the gate itself. The gate’s Solidity Flag is set to a value of 02\. This property determines how solid an object is.

*[Image omitted]*

For comparison, the gate we clip through for [SCS](#skull-cliff-skip-\(scs\)) has a Solidity Flag value of 00\. This explains why it is easier to clip through.

*[Image omitted]*

Here is an example of the effect these different values have. In this video, the properties of the gate have been edited. The left side of the gate has a Solidity Flag of 02 while the right has a value of 00\. [https\://www\.youtube.com/watch?v=iiiurbDiK-k](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iiiurbDiK-k)

**NOTE:** These properties can also explain why Clip Clip Gust Skip is inconsistent. The Solidity Flag of that gate is also set at 02\.

In addition to the Solidity Flags, the trigger that normally opens the gate when you approach it is slightly further away from the Hedge 1 gate than it is from the SCS gate. 

What we believe happens is Scooby clips through these gates just enough to touch the trigger on the other side that opens them, thus allowing us to pass through.

## **Glitchy Hazards**

Besides Softlocks, there are a few glitches that can happen in the game that can kill you and ruin your runs and there isn’t much you can do to control whether or not these things happen.

- Depending on whether the tide is up or down, you can land on a brown sinkable boat and it might just indiscriminately kill you despite the fact that you are not touching the water and the boat has not sunk yet. This is most commonly a problem in Coast 3\.  
    
- If you try to land on top of one of the crushing Coast Boxes in Coast 2 while they are moving up or down, it can kill you because it registers as you being crushed. You can also get killed by running against the side of the box while it’s down.   
    
- In Aghast 1, you can hit weird death planes while sliding down the boats. Sometimes your momentum can fling you off the boat and kill you.  
    
- At the end of Aghast 1, landing on the very last ledge before the blue boat can kill you, similar to landing on brown sinkable boats.  
    
- In Lighthouse 3… just watch the tide and pray you can make it through.
